HERE’S THE CATCH
From the inside, it’s genuinely hard to tell which of the four pillars is the one holding you back.

They can all look like they’re working, or you feel like all of them need work.

So you don’t guess. You score.

In the workshop, you and your team rate your business across the four pillars, one at a time. For each, you answer a short set of questions (the same ones a paid audit would start with) and score yourself out of five.

By the end, you’re holding four numbers. Optimising the lowest one will have the largest impact on your revenue.

That’s your first move. Not a list of twelve competing priorities.
