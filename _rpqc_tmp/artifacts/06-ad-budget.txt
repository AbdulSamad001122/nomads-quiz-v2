Design notes for the team
Spend and visitors stay the same, so they sit up top as two chips instead of repeating in both table columns.
Revenue is drawn as two bars on one scale, with a dashed line marking the $17,500 ad spend. Today’s $11,300 stops short of the line; the goal’s $73,800 clears it four times over.
ROAS (0.6× and 4.2×) sits as a small label next to each bar instead of its own row.
The +$62,500 card shows its working ($73,800 − $11,300) and ends with a “Watch the workshop” button that links to the separate workshop page.
