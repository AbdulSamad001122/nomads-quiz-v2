TEAM APPROACH
And Here's Why I Recommend Doing It With Your Team
ONE TEAM. ONE VIEW. ONE SHARED GOAL.
THE PROBLEM
The four scores live in four different heads.
Your content lead knows the traffic: which posts pulled 40,000 impressions last month.
Sales knows what happens on the calls: the same three questions every prospect asks.
Your email person knows the list: who opens, who clicks, who’s gone quiet.
Each team knows its own numbers. But without seeing how they pull on each other, you can’t tell which one is holding revenue back and which one is amplifying it.
SO…

Which is exactly why a slow month feels like a mystery.

THE WORKSHOP
The workshop is the first time your team looks at one shared goal.
MARKETING SAYS
“Reach is up 40% this quarter, and we booked 30% more calls.”
SALES SAYS
“Half those calls weren’t a fit. I spent most of them explaining what we do.”

Both are telling the truth. In the workshop, both numbers go on the same scorecard, and everyone is finally on the same page (literally, and figuratively!), looking at one number: what each visitor is worth.

Too Close to See It
But when you’re workshopping this with your team: your team is too close to the product, to sales, to the customers.
Closeness Creates Blind Spots
That closeness is a strength on most days. But when you’re that near to it, it’s also the reason the obvious thing hides in plain sight.
WHERE I COME IN

I help you and your team read the story your numbers are telling, the one that’s hard to hear from the inside.
