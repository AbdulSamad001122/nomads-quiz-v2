Design notes for the team
Hero: the before and after numbers are the hero. $0.75 sits in red as “Today”, $4.92 in green as “Your goal”, with the 6.6× jump between them. They should be the first thing anyone sees.
Five-metric table: every cell in “Your new goal” is green with a tick, so “until they’re green” is literally true. Today’s cells carry a Red / Close label as well as the colour. All rows are the same height, and the table fits one screen on desktop.
RPV row: moved to the bottom as the total, because the metrics above feed it. One thing to check: the headline says “all 5 metrics”, but the table shows four (visitors is the fifth). Either add a visitors row or change the line to match.
Red-metric line: “Your red metric is what’s keeping your RPV at $0.75 instead of $4.92” now names the red metrics and shows each one’s number and goal.
Workshop button: sits right after the red metrics, where the reader wants the answer, and again in the nav.
Workshop page: the video comes off the results page and lives on its own “Watch the Workshop” page. Every workshop button links there. Why hasn’t that page been built yet?
Next: the “About your ad budget” section gets the same treatment so the data reads clearly.
