Design notes for the team
Same copy, with a sneak peek. The section keeps its copy. Next to it, a looping preview of the real calculator types in a channel, clicks “Add this channel”, and drops it into the channel ledger with its Revenue Per Visitor™.
Tied to the $1.5M promise: the preview opens on the calculator’s own question, “What would it take to add $1.5M…”, and the last column, “RPV for +$125K/month”, stays blurred and locked, so the answer is the reason to click.
The payoff: a “Most profitable” and “Least profitable” pair sits under the ledger from the very first second (it never hides while the preview loops), and those rows tint green and red. The button sits centred below, in Stone.
Field and column names follow the live calculator (“Visitors” is shortened from its visitor field). The channels and numbers are examples; the blurred values are placeholders, not the calculator’s maths.
Brand colours: Midnight Blue section, Ice Blue accents, Stone button, lightest Stone window bar. Green and red only on the most/least profitable callouts.
