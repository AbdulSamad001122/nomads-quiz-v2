Design notes for the team
Version 2, alongside version 1. Same copy as the live page. The red metric labels follow the results table; the completion one is reworded to “book a call or add to cart … end up buying”.
The red-metric line is the loudest thing: a white box with a red border and a pulsing red dot, $0.75 in red, $4.92 in Midnight Blue.
Red metrics plotted to quadrants, no tapping: only the red metrics show, under the red-metric line. Each one carries the numbers of the quadrants that move it, and those quadrant tiles carry the red number back and get a red outline.
Mapping to confirm: Q1 → opt-in rate; Q2 and Q3 → buying action and completion; Q4 → Revenue Per Subscriber™. This is my reading of the framework, so please check it before build.
Brand colours only: Midnight Blue background, Ice Blue quadrant tiles, white and Deep Purple text. Red and yellow appear only as metric status colours.
